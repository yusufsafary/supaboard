How BOARD moves

Only two flows exist. Winning positions claim BOARD out of a Claim contract pool, and a project wallet holds a reserve line for later decisions such as a liquidity pool. Positions themselves cost BNB on paid rungs; that BNB forwards to a project wallet and never enters a prize pot. Prizes are always BOARD from a funded pool, never other players' BNB.

Claim math

Share per winning position = 6,250,000 BOARD ÷ winning positions in that Call. Ten winners take 625,000 each; a hundred winners take 62,500 each; a single winner takes a whole pool. A claim fee in BNB applies per claim and forwards instantly to a project wallet; a fee never reduces a BOARD share.
