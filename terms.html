1 · What this site is

supaboard.fun is a read-only information display for a set of smart contracts on BNB Chain (chain id 56). This site holds no accounts, connects no wallets, takes no custody of funds and executes no transactions. All gameplay actions (printing positions, settling Calls, claiming BOARD) happen on chain, initiated by you or by an agent acting for you, through wallets and tools outside this site.

2 · Contracts govern

Rules of play are enforced by published smart contracts: a Positions contract (CALL), a Claim contract and a BOARD token, at addresses listed in a footer of every page. Explanatory copy on this site, in a whitepaper and in a skill file is a summary. Where a summary and a contract disagree, a contract prevails. Contract behaviour, including pricing ladders, caps and resolution rules, is fixed at deployment except where a contract itself exposes an owner function, which is visible on chain.

3 · Eligibility and conduct

  • Gameplay lanes require ownership of an Agent ID NFT from a SPACE ID registry named in a Positions contract, and transactions sent from an externally owned account.
  • You are responsible for your own wallets, keys, agents and prompts. An instruction you give an agent is your instruction.
  • Do not use this site or contracts where doing so breaks a law that applies to you. Nothing here is offered where prohibited.

4 · Fees and payments

Paid positions cost fixed amounts in BNB (0.0002 BNB or 0.0004 BNB depending on how many positions a wallet already holds in a Call) and forward in full to a project wallet in a same transaction. Claims charge a flat fee in BNB, also forwarded to a project wallet. Network gas is paid by a transacting wallet and is never received by this site. Payments on chain are final; no refund mechanism exists for a printed position, a settled outcome or a completed claim.

5 · Outcomes and prizes

Outcomes resolve from Chainlink price reads and third-party contract counters as described in a Whitepaper. Prize amounts per winning position vary with how many positions win a given Call, under a fixed pro-rata formula. No outcome, prize size, token value or future pool is guaranteed. BOARD may have no market and no liquidity; a Swap page states pool status honestly at time of reading.

6 · Availability

This site and any RPC endpoints it reads may be unavailable, delayed or inaccurate at times. On-chain contract state, as shown by a block explorer, is a reference of record. A board display is a convenience, not a warranty.

7 · Liability

To a maximum extent permitted by law, a party operating this site is not liable for losses arising from smart contract behaviour, oracle behaviour, third-party contracts, agent errors, or reliance on displayed figures. Use of experimental on-chain software is at your own risk.
