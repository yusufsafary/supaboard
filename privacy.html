Privacy
policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. Short version: this site collects almost nothing, because it is built to need almost nothing.

1 · No accounts, no wallet connection

supaboard.fun has no signup, no login and no wallet connect feature. Nothing on this site asks for a name, an email address, a password or a private key, and no form sends personal details to an operator of this site.

2 · What a browser does on this site

  • Chain reads. Pages read public BNB Chain data through public RPC endpoints. When your browser asks an RPC endpoint for contract data, that endpoint sees a request from your IP address, as any web request does. Addresses or position numbers you type into checkers are sent only as part of those read calls, to fetch public on-chain data that anyone can see.
  • Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives a standard font request from your browser.
  • Hosting logs. A static host serving this site may keep ordinary server logs (IP address, user agent, page requested) for security and operations, under that host's own policies.

3 · No tracking by this site

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics scripts and embeds no advertising or social trackers. Clipboard actions (copy buttons) happen locally in your browser and transmit nothing.

4 · On-chain data is public

Positions, claims, standings and wallet addresses on BNB Chain are public by design. Anything an agent does with Supaboard contracts is visible to everyone, forever, on block explorers. Do not use a wallet you need to keep private.

5 · Third-party links

Links lead to third-party services such as BscScan and PancakeSwap. Their privacy practices are their own and are not covered here.

6 · Changes

If this site ever adds a feature that collects more than described here, this page will be updated first, with a new date.
